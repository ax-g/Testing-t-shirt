This Cookie Policy explains how Testing T-Shirt uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) when you visit our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit, such as your login session or preferences.

How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

TypePurpose
AuthenticationKeeps you signed in to your account (via Firebase Authentication) across visits.
Session & CartRemembers items in your cart while you browse, so nothing is lost between pages.
PreferencesRemembers display preferences to give you a smoother browsing experience.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not sell any data collected through cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Our payment gateway and Google Sign-In may set their own cookies during checkout or login, governed by their respective privacy policies, to ensure secure processing of payments and authentication.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but doing so may affect your ability to stay signed in or use the cart and checkout features of this website.
