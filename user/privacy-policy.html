Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Testing T-Shirt ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website to browse and purchase products.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication details when you sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Order information: delivery address, phone number, order history, and payment method selected.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, products browsed, and cart activity, used to improve your shopping experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate order updates, confirmations, and support responses
  • To maintain your saved addresses and order history for your convenience
  • To improve our website and product offerings

Payment Information

We do not store your card, UPI, or banking details on our servers. Online payments are processed through a secure third-party payment gateway. Cash on Delivery orders do not require any payment information to be shared with us in advance.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only with:

  • Our payment gateway provider, to process online payments securely
  • Delivery/logistics partners, to fulfill your order
  • Firebase (Google), our backend infrastructure provider, which stores and secures your account and order data

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including Firebase Authentication and access-controlled databases, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You can view and manage your saved addresses and order history at any time by signing in to your account. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
